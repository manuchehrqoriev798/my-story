# Part Two

## The Lessons

*Nine chapters. Each one stands on its own.*

Everyone can read motivational quotes and feel something for an hour. When
you take a lesson out of a conversation with a real person, it lands
differently. Everything in this part came from a real person, and most of
them are still alive and still would not think of themselves as teachers.

The initials are theirs. The mistakes in understanding them are mine.
