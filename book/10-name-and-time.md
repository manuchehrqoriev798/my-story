## Half Your Life Works For the Name

### Chapter Eight

> Half of your life works for the name, and the other half the name works
> for you.
> — D.

There is a point, somewhere in the middle, where the direction of effort
reverses. Before it, everything you do is deposited: no interest, no
visibility, frequently no acknowledgement. After it, the accumulated thing
starts producing on its own. Doors open before you knock. People vouch for
you in rooms you were never in.

The difficulty is that the curve is invisible from the first half. Someone
four years into building something and seeing nothing return has exactly the
same view as someone four years into building the wrong thing. The data
looks identical. Only time separates them.

Which is why the earlier chapters matter more than this one. Direction is
checkable now. Return is not.

> It is not how many years in your life, but how much life in your years.
> — A.L.

Duration is not the variable. Almost nobody optimising their life is
actually optimising duration, though many people believe they are, and it
distorts everything downstream: the job held for safety, the decision
deferred for another year, the risk not taken at the exact age when risk is
cheapest.

> Go to sleep with a dream, and wake up with a goal.
> — L.A.

A dream is a direction with no coordinates. A goal is a coordinate with a
date. You need both and they operate at different times of day, and the
translation between them happens overnight, every night, and that is the
entire practice.
